# Observability

Observability tools help you understand behavior in production and during experimentation.

Useful capabilities include:

- tracing
- prompt and response logging
- experiment comparison
- metric dashboards
- alerting and monitoring

## Common Questions

- What changed between two runs?
- Which prompts or datasets are causing regressions?
- Which failures are new versus already known?
- Are costs or latencies drifting over time?

Observability is what turns evaluation into an operational discipline.
