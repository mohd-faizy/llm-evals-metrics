# Frameworks

Common evaluation frameworks include:

- OpenAI Evals
- LangSmith
- Ragas
- DeepEval
- Promptfoo
- TruLens
- Phoenix
- Inspect

## How To Compare Them

- what input formats they accept
- how they execute tests
- what metrics they support
- whether they support judges or human review
- how they integrate with CI/CD and dashboards

## Selection Rule

Pick the framework that reduces friction for your current workflow. Do not start by asking which one is "best" in the abstract.
